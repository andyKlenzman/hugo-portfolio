As part of a 22-week intensive program with [Nucamp](https://www.nucamp.co/bootcamp-overview/full-stack-web-mobile-development), I was tasked with building a web application from scratch for our final project. I wanted to create something fun and unconventional that could help people connect in a new way.

The result was **T-share**, a shirt with a printed QR code that acts as a playful conversation starter. When someone scans the code, they are directed to a customizable experience on the web app. Users could configure their shirt to:

- Play games
- Tell jokes
- Display personal announcements
- Even ask someone out on a date

I built the **frontend with React**, the **backend with Node.js/Express**, and used **MongoDB** for storing user content. The project emphasized both technical full-stack implementation and imaginative user-experience design.

Beyond the demo, I also printed several T-shares and wore them in real situations. They attracted a lot of attention and sparked unique interactions. Some of the most memorable uses included:

1. A "Reddit Thread" feature where friends and family left farewell messages before I moved to Germany.
2. Using the "ask on a date" option to successfully get someone's phone number.
3. Making someone's day by showing a message like "You are beautiful" to a curious stranger.
